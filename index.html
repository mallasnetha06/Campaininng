How to discover high-value researcher emails, optimize deliverability, and send campaigns with zero technical complexity.

Ready-to-Use Cold Email Outreach Templates

Copy directly into Gmail or Outlook

Template 1: Academic Research & Collaboration Outreach

Subject: Question regarding your recent work on [Research Topic / Article Title] Dear Dr. [Author Last Name], I came across your recent publication titled "[Article Title]" in [Journal Name] and wanted to reach out regarding your findings in [Topic]. Our team has developed a specialized protocol/resource specifically tailored for researchers focusing on [Field], and we thought this might be directly applicable to your lab's ongoing projects. Would you be open to a brief 5-minute conversation or receiving a 1-page summary of our data? Best regards, [Your Name] [Your Organization]

Template 2: Product / Reagent Introduction for Lab Heads

Subject: Complimentary sample for your research on [Topic] Dear Dr. [Author Last Name], Given your recent paper on [Article Title], I wanted to let you know that we are offering complimentary test kits/evaluations to leading research groups working on [Disease / Target]. Researchers using our solution have reported significantly improved yield and reproducibility in comparable workflows. Could I mail a complimentary sample kit to your department at [Affiliation / University]? Kind regards, [Your Name] [Your Company]

Template 3: Webinar or Symposium Invitation

Subject: Invitation: Upcoming expert panel on [Topic] Dear Dr. [Author Last Name], We are hosting an upcoming virtual roundtable bringing together leaders in [Research Field] to discuss recent breakthroughs and translation challenges. Knowing your recent contributions to [Topic], we would be delighted to have you attend. You can view the full agenda and register here: [Link] Warm regards, [Your Name] [Your Organization]

Deliverability & Outreach Best Practices

  • Always Use the BCC Field: When sending announcements to multiple academic contacts from Outlook or Gmail, paste addresses into the BCC: field to protect recipient privacy.
  • Personalize the Opening Line: Academic researchers receive dozens of emails daily. Referencing their journal publication or research subject dramatically increases reply rates.
  • Keep Cold Outreach Under 150 Words: Clearly state who you are, why you are reaching out, and provide a single clear call-to-action.
  • Respect Opt-Outs: Always include a polite closing: "If you'd rather not hear about future genomics tools, please let me know and I will remove your address."
